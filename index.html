Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

Size Small to medium

Personality Curious and sociable

Care Take daily walks and let it sniff in safe places.

Golden Retriever

Size Medium to large

Personality Friendly and eager to play

Care Make time for exercise and brush its coat regularly.

Poodle

Size Toy, miniature or standard

Personality Alert and quick to learn

Care Brush its curls often and give it games to figure out.
